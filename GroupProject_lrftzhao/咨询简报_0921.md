## Consultation brief — Group [X] · 21 Sep 2026

### 1. User + decision → problem statement (one sentence)

*At 48 hours after ICU admission we rank patients for senior clinician review (in-hospital death, T1), estimate the cohort's remaining hospital bed-days (T2), and show what the second day of data was worth (T3).* The only action claimed for T1 is a review triggered for patients the model ranks high-risk but the team has not flagged.

### 2. Data + baseline → primary metric

| | Baseline we must beat | Our result (test, evaluated once) | Primary metric and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | SAPS-I alone: AUC 0.642 (SOFA 0.622; majority class 0.5) | Logistic regression **0.847** (0.825–0.867); XGBoost 0.855, CIs overlap | **AUC-ROC** — the review queue is a ranking problem. Threshold table and calibration curve reported alongside; no probability is claimed without calibration. |
| T2 | Predict the training median (8 days): MAE 7.24 | Lasso on log1p(remaining LOS): **MAE 6.56** (6.15–7.04); cohort bed-days within −0.2% with smearing correction | **MAE (days)** for individual error; **Σŷ vs Σy** for the capacity claim. Two read-outs of one model, never mixed. |
| T3 | 48 h model | 24 h model AUC 0.817 (−0.03); 36 h 0.840 | Fair comparison with the 24 h SAPS-I score; the last 12 h buy 0.007. |

Pipeline discipline: one stratified 60/20/20 split fixed before modelling; imputation and scaling fitted on training folds only; hyper-parameters on validation only; test once per task. Fully reproducible (`run_all.sh`, 16 scripts, ~12 min; identical outputs on two machines).

### 3. What we have beyond the minimum

- **Unseen-data test:** leave-one-ICU-type-out — ranking holds (AUC 0.82–0.86), calibration breaks (risk over-estimated 84% in CSRU, mortality 4.9% vs 19.8% in MICU).
- **Label sensitivity:** "alive at discharge" → "alive 30 days after discharge": prevalence 14.2% → 17.1%, ranking unchanged (0.847 → 0.843).
- **Process vs physiology:** a model that sees only which tests were ordered reaches AUC 0.74 — we quantify the care-process component rather than claim pure physiology.
- **Missed-death autopsy:** the 44 deaths missed at sensitivity 0.90 look like survivors in the first 48 h (GCS 15, no lactate ordered) and do not die later.
- Interpretability for every model: odds ratios, grouped permutation importance, partial dependence, Lasso coefficients.

### 4. Metric + blocker → what we need from you

**Blocker 1 — the alert budget.** Sensitivity 0.90 flags 43 of 100 admissions; 0.80 flags 28. Is "a cheap second opinion" still an honest description at 43%? Which budget should the main claim be built on?

**Blocker 2 — the capacity claim.** We can report remaining *hospital* bed-days at cohort level (−0.2% on test) but not ICU beds (no ICU discharge time in the data) and not individual dates (MAE 6.6 days, long-stay tail dominates). Is a cohort-level claim worth a slide, or should T2 be presented purely as the regression counterpart of T1?

**Blocker 3 — calibration across units.** Given the CSRU result, we intend to present the model as a ranking tool only and state that probabilities must be re-calibrated locally. Is that the right level of claim for this course, or do you expect a re-calibration step (e.g. Platt / isotonic on the held-out unit) to be shown?

### 5. Logistics we need confirmed

1. Code submission deadline and format (repository? notebook? both?).
2. Which presentation batch we are in (19 Oct or 26 Oct).
3. Minutes per group, and whether Q&A is separate.
